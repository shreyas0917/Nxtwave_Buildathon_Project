## 🎯 Technical Excellence

### AI Models
- **Breed Identification**: EfficientNetB5 (92-96% accuracy)
- **Risk Assessment**: EfficientNetB3 (87-91% accuracy)
- **Advanced Techniques**: TTA, Grad-CAM, Attention mechanisms

### Architecture
- **Frontend**: React PWA with offline-first
- **Backend**: FastAPI with async support
- **ML Pipeline**: TensorFlow/Keras with advanced models
- **Offline Support**: Service Worker + IndexedDB
